ARTICLEHUB POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information ArticleHub may collect, how it may be used, and the measures taken to protect it.

Effective date: November 12, 2026 • Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

ArticleHub is committed to respecting the privacy of individuals who visit or use its website and services.

This Privacy Policy describes how information relating to users may be collected, processed, stored, and protected when they interact with ArticleHub.

By using the platform, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available and how you interact with ArticleHub, the platform may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Username.
  • Account credentials, handled through the authentication system when implemented.
  • Account role and registration status.

Content and Communication Information

  • Articles and other content submitted by authors.
  • Messages or enquiries sent through contact forms.
  • Feedback and communications relating to platform services.

Technical Information

  • Browser and device information.
  • Operating system information.
  • Basic website usage and diagnostic information, where enabled.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through ArticleHub may be used for legitimate platform operations, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing access to available platform features.
  • Reviewing author registration requests.
  • Managing submitted articles and publications.
  • Responding to enquiries and support requests.
  • Maintaining website functionality and security.
  • Improving platform performance and user experience.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Passwords and Account Security

Account credentials are sensitive information and should never be shared with other individuals.

When authentication services are implemented, password handling should use appropriate security practices, including secure storage and protected authentication processes.

ArticleHub will not intentionally request your existing account password through ordinary email communication.

Users should notify the platform administration team if they suspect unauthorized access to their accounts.

5. Cookies and Website Preferences

ArticleHub may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve the browsing experience.

Depending on implementation, these technologies may:

  • Maintain necessary website functionality.
  • Remember user preferences, such as theme selection.
  • Support website performance and usage analysis.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

ArticleHub does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary for legitimate platform operations, including:

  • With authorized personnel responsible for platform administration.
  • With service providers supporting hosting, security, or other platform functions, where applicable.
  • Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Where necessary to protect users, the platform, or the rights of others.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored using systems and services selected to support ArticleHub's operations.

Personal information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, account status, and operational or legal requirements.

8. Data Security

ArticleHub aims to apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no website or electronic transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their devices, accounts, and login credentials.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and circumstances, users may have rights relating to their personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities where applicable.
  • Raise concerns about how their information is handled.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

ArticleHub may provide links to external websites or integrate third-party services.

Such services may operate under their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

ArticleHub does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. Users should review their policies before providing personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

ArticleHub is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Where age-related restrictions apply, the platform will follow the relevant legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ArticleHub introduces new features, changes its data practices, or needs to reflect applicable legal requirements.

Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

13. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact the ArticleHub administration team.
