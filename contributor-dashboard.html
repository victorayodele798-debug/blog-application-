PUBLISHING RESOURCES

Author Guidelines

Important principles for publishing quality content on ArticleHub.

01

Originality and Accuracy

Submit original work and ensure that factual claims are accurate and supported by reliable sources.

02

Clear Writing

Use clear language, meaningful paragraphs, descriptive headings, and an appropriate structure.

03

Respect Copyright

Do not publish material that belongs to others without permission or appropriate attribution.

04

Use Appropriate Images

Only submit images you have the right to use. Provide meaningful descriptions for accessibility.

05

Editorial Review

Submitted articles may be reviewed before publication. An article is not publicly available until approved.

06

Professional Conduct

Avoid harmful, misleading, discriminatory, or abusive content. Respect readers and other contributors.
